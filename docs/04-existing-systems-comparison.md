# 4. Existing systems comparison

Four systems are compared against eight criteria: BUKI Kazakhstan, Wyzant, Preply and Superprof US. These cover a local service, international lesson marketplaces and a contact/subscription directory model. Public official pages were inspected on 20 October 2026. This is a desk comparison, not an account-based usability or purchase test. Regional editions differ. **Unverified** means the selected sources did not establish the capability; it does not mean it is absent.

## Implications

BUKI contradicts the archive's claim that there is no relevant local marketplace. Russian discovery, tenge pricing, qualification checks and exam subjects cannot be claimed as unique TutorGo features.

The proposed contribution is an inspectable engineering implementation of availability, booking requests, tutor acceptance, sandbox payment and transaction-linked reviews. Whether this is preferable to BUKI or informal search remains a hypothesis. Interviews and evaluation must establish value; a feature matrix alone does not demonstrate demand or superiority.

Adopt clear verification explanations, visible price/availability and review provenance. Evaluate an explicit booking lifecycle against contact-based arrangements. Do not infer a competitor lacks payments or video when the selected sources do not document them.
