Question 1: How do you usually search for tutors when preparing for exams like UNT, and how long does it take?
Answer: I usually look on Instagram, OLX, or ask friends in Telegram chats. It usually takes about 3 to 5 days because I have to message around 5 or 6 different tutors on WhatsApp just to check their prices, schedule, and if they actually know the exam format.

Question 2: What is the most annoying part about finding a tutor through social media or classified ads?
Answer: You can't verify if they are actually good. Anyone can post a nice picture and write that their students got high scores, but there are no real verified diplomas or honest review systems. You basically just risk your money and hope for the best.

Question 3: If there was a platform with verified diplomas, clear prices, and 2-click booking, would you use it?
Answer: Yeah, definitely. It would save me from sending endless WhatsApp messages and help me find a legit tutor much faster.
